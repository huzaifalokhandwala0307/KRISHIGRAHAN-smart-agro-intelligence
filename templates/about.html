About KrishiGrahan

Smart Agro Intelligence Platform for Modern Farmers

Platform Overview

What is KrishiGrahan?

KrishiGrahan is an AI-powered smart agriculture platform designed to empower farmers with data-driven insights. By leveraging advanced Machine Learning and Computer Vision, the platform provides tailored crop recommendations, customized fertilizer suggestions, and real-time plant disease diagnosis. Our goal is to reduce input costs, maximize harvest yields, and promote sustainable farming practices.

Workflow

How It Works

🌾

1. Crop Recommendation

Analyze N-P-K soil nutrients, temperature, humidity, pH, and rainfall to discover the ideal crop to sow in your field.

🧪

2. Fertilizer Suggestion

Calculate the exact chemical and organic fertilizers needed to replenish nutrients in your soil based on the target crop.

🔬

3. Disease Detection

Upload leaf photos or take a snapshot using your phone's camera to instantly detect plant diseases using deep learning neural networks.

System Details

Technology Stack & Team

AI & Machine Learning

Powered by Scikit-Learn (Random Forest classifiers) and TensorFlow/Keras (MobileNetV2 deep neural network for Computer Vision).

Backend & Web Framework

Built using Python, Flask, and Pandas to run fast real-time model inferences and render HTML dynamically.

Frontend Design

Designed using HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, CSS3 grids, and responsive Glassmorphic elements for desktop and mobile devices.

Built By

Developed with love by Smart Agro Intelligence to support Indian farmers and make technology accessible to all.
